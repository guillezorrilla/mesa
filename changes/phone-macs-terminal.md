Several Macs on one iPhone, and terminals from the phone: the iPhone app pairs with more than one Mac or profile, lists all their sessions in Agents with a filter per Mac, and unpairs each on its own; a plain terminal session opens with its live output, a command line and a key row (Ctrl, Esc, Tab, arrows, Enter), and `mesa type` and `mesa keys` do the same from the CLI.
