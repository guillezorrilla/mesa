Pull requests on your phone: the Work tab's PRs list shows the pull requests that need you in Mesa's buckets, and a PR's screen shows its checks, description, your threads and drafts, and its files in the diff viewer; from there you can start a review session, reply to or resolve a thread, discard a draft, send a failed check to the session on the branch, and merge. On your Mac, Resolve on a PR's threads and `mesa prs resolve` do the same for a thread.
