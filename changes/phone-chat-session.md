A session on your phone now reads like a messages app: the conversation runs oldest to newest with the latest reply just above the reply bar, your own messages as bubbles on the right and the agent's replies full width. It opens at the bottom and stays there as replies arrive; scroll up and it holds still, with a Jump to latest button that counts what came in. Scrolling to the top loads older replies, and queued replies, the pending prompt and the session's output (its last lines, tap to see all) sit at the bottom of the same flow. Your messages and older replies need the Mac on this release; an older Mac still shows its last 5 replies.
