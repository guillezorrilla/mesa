Impact shows what Mesa decided and kept for you and what happened next: a one-sentence summary, then each session's moments (a question the rules missed, a vault note brought into a prompt, a risky command stopped, notes kept), who decided and how sure, and what the rules alone would have said. Open it from the title bar beside Cost and Help, a project's Impact tab, Mesa's part in session details, Search Mesa, or `mesa impact`; `mesa decisions log` lists every decision behind it.
