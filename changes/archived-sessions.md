Archived sessions in the app: the Sessions tab has an Archived section at the bottom, with its count, listing each archived session by title, project and when it was archived. Unarchive brings a session back live in its project, resumed, and Delete permanently removes it; archiving a session shows a toast with Undo, which does the same as Unarchive. `mesa sessions --archived` lists them, and `mesa unarchive --resume` resumes one.
