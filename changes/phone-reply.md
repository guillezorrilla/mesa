Reply to a session from your phone: Mesa's iPhone app, coming next, gets a reply bar on each session with your saved prompts, a photo or screenshot, drafts that wait for you, and a menu to stop, resume, fork, hand off, rename or archive it. A reply to a working session waits on your Mac until its turn ends, and the guardrail checks every reply as it does `mesa send`.
